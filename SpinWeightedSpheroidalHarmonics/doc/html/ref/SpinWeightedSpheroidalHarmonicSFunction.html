SpinWeightedSpheroidalHarmonicSFunction

SpinWeightedSpheroidalHarmonicSFunction[…]

represents a solution to the spin-weighted spheroidal equation.

Details and Options

  • A SpinWeightedSpheroidalHarmonicSFunction is created when using SpinSpinWeightedSpheroidalHarmonicS without specifying values for θ and ϕ.
  • A SpinSpinWeightedSpheroidalHarmonicSFunction may be treated as a numeric function of two arguments, θ and ϕ.
  • The parameters s, l, m and γ, the method used, and pre-computed expansion coefficients are stored inside the SpinWeightedSpheroidalHarmonicSFunction.
  • Numerical evaluation for specific θ and ϕ is done by summing the pre-computed expansion coefficients times the basis functions (the particular basis depends on the Method).
