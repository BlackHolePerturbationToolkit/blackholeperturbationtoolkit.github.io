SpinWeightedSphericalHarmonicY

SpinWeightedSphericalHarmonicY[s, l, m, θ, ϕ] gives the spin-weighted spherical harmonic of spin-weight s, degree l and order m.

Details and Options

  • SpinWeightedSphericalHarmonicY satisfies the spin-weighted spherical equation,
  • The eigenvalue, λ for the spin-weighted spherical equation is given by λ = l(l+1) - s(s+1).
  • SpinWeightedSphericalHarmonicY is unit-normalised on the two-sphere, .
  • For s=0, SpinWeightedSphericalHarmonicY is equivalent to SphericalHarmonicY.
  • For certain special arguments, SpinWeightedSphericalHarmonicY automatically evaluates to exact values.
  • SpinWeightedSphericalHarmonicY can be evaluated to arbitrary numerical precision.
  • SpinWeightedSphericalHarmonicY automatically threads over lists.
